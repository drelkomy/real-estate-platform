# Product Roadmap — Draft

## Phase 0: Discovery and foundation
- Confirm business model, countries, currencies, user types and moderation.
- Initialize Laravel 13 / PHP 8.5.12 project.
- Integrate Docker services and CI checks.
- Establish auth, roles, policies, audit log and localization.

## Phase 1: Property and listing MVP
- Property types and property data model.
- Sale/rent listings and publication lifecycle.
- Media uploads and processing.
- Location hierarchy.
- Public listing pages and search filters.
- Owner/agency dashboard.
- Admin moderation.

## Phase 2: Engagement
- Favorites.
- Inquiries and contact workflows.
- Viewing requests.
- Email/database notifications.
- Reporting and abuse controls.

## Phase 3: Monetization
- Plans/subscriptions only after business rules are approved.
- Listing quotas and featured listings.
- Payment provider integration after country/provider/legal review.

## Phase 4: Scale and optimization
- Search optimization based on measured data.
- CDN/object storage.
- Observability and operational dashboards.
- Read replicas/search engine only if metrics justify them.

All phases are provisional. Do not implement future phases without explicit task approval.
